Refuting Misconceptions

by Abi Abd al-Mu'izz Muhammad Ali Ferkous al-Jazairi

The book Al-Fath ar-Rabbani shows you how to dismantle the spurious arguments at the heart of modern trials (fitnas), which are often based on twisting the rulings of scholars and altering the meaning of the texts. It provides clear methodologies for understanding Islamic texts correctly and defending against misinterpretations.

The Disease and the Cure
الداء والدواء

Spiritual Healing & Guidance

by Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya رحمه الله

The book diagnoses the spiritual and psychological 'diseases' that affect the human heart and soul, such as sin, destructive desires, heedlessness, and doubt. It then prescribes the 'cures' for these ailments using remedies drawn directly from the Qur'an and the traditions of the Prophet Muhammad (Sunnah). A comprehensive guide to spiritual purification and healing.
